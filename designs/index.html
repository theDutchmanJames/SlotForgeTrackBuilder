Research and decisions

Reviewed 29 October 2026. Exact SlotForge Timing dark-theme values: background #171b21, panel #222831, text #e8e6e1, muted #98a0ab, line #343b47, accent #e5484d. Segoe UI, 8 px cards, 6 px buttons, selected tab underline and standard soft-lift depth 0.35. The reference CSS is extracted directly from the repository; this is a static design document, not the app.

  • Source theme and component CSS — blob 704356ee7bd1da91b7cc3c877dfe3e574dae1bad.
  • Source subtab styling — tinted surfaces, coloured left edge, selected underline.
  • Carrera standard straight — 20020509 contains four 345 mm pieces; common passive track compatibility covers Evolution, Digital 132 and Digital 124.
  • Carrera R1/60 curve — 20020571 contains three 60° pieces.
  • Carrera 2021 manufacturer catalogue (hosted copy) — core curve/straight/border SKU mapping. Published short straights are 115 mm and 86 mm. These are published nominal values, not a tolerance specification.
  • Carrera 2022 manufacturer catalogue (hosted copy) — 20598 contains two right and two left shoulder end pieces. Their footprints must be modelled separately.
  • Carrera 2020 manufacturer catalogue (hosted copy) — 30343/30345 are directional lane-change assemblies of two 345 mm sections (690 mm overall). Their exact internal route shape remains unverified.

What still needs measurement

No complete manufacturer engineering drawing was located. Provisional drawing parameters are 198 mm track width; 99 mm lane spacing and shoulder width; inner radii 198, 396, 594 and 792 mm. These are not treated as verified specifications. Exact track width, slot centres, each radius, quarter-straight length, connector offsets, borders, end caps and hardware enclosures need measured confirmation. Decorative dimensions are also provisional.

Geometry and inventory rules

  • One geometry family for shared passive track, with explicit electrical/system restrictions for active pieces. Physical fit does not imply electrical compatibility.
  • Track, shoulder and end-cap outlines all contribute to the footprint. Borders add no driving distance. Guardrails and supports need their own envelopes where they project beyond the road.
  • Nested curves share a centre. R1/60 aligns with two R2/30, two R3/30 and four R4/15 pieces. Do not insert overlapping borders between adjacent track surfaces.
  • Ordinary curve reversal swaps endpoint/lane mapping. Directional digital pieces need an explicit valid counterpart, not an unrestricted graphical mirror.
  • Catalogue SKU identifies a purchase package; geometry identifies an individual piece. 20509 and 20601 are different package quantities for standard straights. Assemblies need component counts.
  • For confirmed flat curves, lane distance is radius × angle in radians. Branching digital routes need route-specific totals. Do not use a smooth-looking invented switch curve as a measured distance.
  • Proposed Track Packs should preserve sources, geometry revision and validation status; saved layouts should embed the definitions they use.
