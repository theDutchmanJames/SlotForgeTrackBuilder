    DESIGN THE SLOTS · BUILD IN WOOD

    Routed Wood

    Plan a routed circuit with editable sections, real slot geometry and your own construction profile.

    Saved routed packs

    Routed profile

    Starting values are editable planning dimensions. Set these to your cars, board, braid or tape. Edge clearances are measured from the first and last slot centres; their meaning follows the routing type. On circular corners, the first slot is the inner slot. Through a flowing S-bend, the inside changes sides.

    Individual pieces have their own wood outline. Tabletop routing places slots in one continuous board; edge clearances are checked against the table boundary. Each section can override this default.

    Recess depth applies to braid. Tape is shown on the surface. For individual pieces, unequal verges can create a step at joins; the checks will flag it.

    Existing straight lengths, circular radii/angles and flowing control points are retained. Connected sections move to keep their joins; changing dimensions can leave a closing gap.

    Profile preview

    No moulded connectors or lane markings. All lanes are generated together from the profile.

    Materials estimate

    Conductors include both sides of every slot, plus your chosen allowance. Sections are design elements, not physical boards.

    Planning checks

      2D footprint checks do not verify vehicle clearance, structural strength or bridge heights.

      Create routed section

      Measure from the bend centre to the innermost slot centre. Every next slot adds one lane spacing.

      Lane squeeze

      Works on straights and bends: smoothly narrows and widens back to normal spacing at both ends. Circular corners become editable flowing approximations; reset restores uniform spacing on that path. Slot numbers stay attached to their paths when mirrored. Check car clearance separately.

      Piece clearances define its wood outline. Tabletop clearances apply to the table boundary. Profile defaults affect new sections; existing sections keep their own settings.
