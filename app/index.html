Create a track piece

Measure from the centre of the bend to the inner road edge, not to a slot. Each next radius adds one road width so the surfaces nest with no gap. Borders need their own space.

Choose a brand only if your connectors physically match. Slot spacing is independent of road width. Dimensions are your measurements, not manufacturer verification.

Matching radius family

Use this artwork to plan a layout even when measurements are incomplete. Planning magnets align approximate road ends; they do not create guide slots or validate physical fit. PDFs include the picture and quantities.

These dimensions guide the drawing and magnets. Use matching road widths within the same track pack. Left/right Hybrid pieces keep their own artwork and handedness.

Add borders
