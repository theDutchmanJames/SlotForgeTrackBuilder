Heights and bridges - 2D planning (v0.3.15)

TRACK PIECES: ANY PACK
Select a piece, then find Heights & bridges in the builder's right panel.
Set Start surface and End surface in mm relative to the table reference level.
Use the same value for a level bridge. Apply heights affects every selected
piece separately; it does not automatically distribute a ramp across a group.
Start/end follow the piece's local A/B direction, not the racing direction.
Selected magnetic endpoints are labelled A/B with heights on the canvas.
Reset heights removes the placement's height settings and returns it to zero.
Undo restores changes. New borders inherit their road's endpoint heights.

The default manufactured deck thickness (10 mm) is an editable estimate.
Routed pieces default to board thickness. Enter actual physical thickness.
Required clearance defaults to 50 mm; choose it for your cars and installation.
A 100 mm upper surface and 12 mm upper deck above a zero-height lower surface
leave 88 mm headroom. The upper deck, not the lower deck, is subtracted.

FREEFORM
In FreeForm Drawing, select points in the drawing or with Selected point.
Open Points > Height & bridge, then Apply height. You can set several selected
anchors together. Heights are stored on anchors and interpolate in the cubic
parameter between them. Insert point preserves that interpolation; drawing a
new endpoint inherits the previous endpoint's height. Locks also protect height.
Set deck thickness and required clearance under Table > Bridge clearance.
Raised crossing paths retain their original lane identity and route order.
They never switch or join lanes just because they cross in the XY drawing.

CHECKS
Ordinary piece footprints are sampled into surface strips. Clearance is
checked across their overlapping area, including ramp height differences.
Shared edges alone are not overlaps. Adjoining magnetic ends must match
height within the normal connection tolerance before joining circuit routes.
FreeForm checks intersecting slot/conductor footprints against interpolated
heights; successful bridge regions get green markers. Folds remain invalid.
These are planning checks, not manufacturing approval. A complex layout can
exceed the check budget; an explicit incomplete-check warning is then shown.
Reference art uses its display bounding box, so clearance is approximate.
Individual piece cut-outs, cars, support structures, bank angles and bridge
ramps' physical curvature are not simulated. Surface thickness is vertical.

SAVING AND EXPORT
Library, JSON, disk and workspace backups retain assigned heights.
PDF reports include heights and clearance notes. SlotForge JSON uses the
version-1 layout envelope with integration contract 1.0.0-draft.9.
Heights do not change plan-view lengths, footprints or slot geometry. They do
not shorten the plan of a tilted plastic piece or add the length of ramps.
SVG/DXF remain XY projections with a warning for height plans: overlapping
bridge paths must not be routed into one flat board. No 3D CAD is generated.
The existing manual Layer order controls still control which artwork paints
on top; height markers and checks do not automatically reorder piece artwork.

Gap filling and closing sections inherit the two fixed endpoint heights.
They interpolate between those heights; this is still a planning ramp, not
ramp construction engineering. End magnets only connect matching heights.
