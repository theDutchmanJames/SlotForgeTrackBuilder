FreeForm Drawing - first iteration (v0.3.15)

Open FreeForm Drawing directly from the main menu. New drawings start with
one lane. Existing drawings retain their saved lane count. The editor keeps
your draft when switching between editor pages; save before reloading or
opening the separate Track Packs catalogue. Save & use in builder asks before
replacing another track. Cancel leaves the builder unchanged.

SIDEBAR NAVIGATION
Points: select a point, then adjust its shape, position, height or squeeze.
Lanes: choose how many lanes to generate and where your drawn lane belongs.
Table: set the base shape, with routing dimensions and bridge clearance
under expandable sections.
Checks: review geometry messages and lane lengths. The status button at
the bottom opens Checks and stays visible while the panel scrolls.
Use Left/Right arrow keys on the tabs to move between them.
Expandable tools use the same bordered folders as the Track Builder
catalogue. Click a folder heading, or press Enter/Space on it, to open or
close it. More than one folder can stay open.

DRAW AND EDIT
Click to add automatically smoothed anchors. Click and drag to set a point's
direction handles. Click the first point, or Close loop, to finish the loop.
You can also start with an oval and reshape it.

Edit points: drag anchors or their coloured handles. Shift-click selects
several anchors; drag a selected anchor to move them together. Ctrl/Cmd+A
selects all anchors. Locked anchors cannot be moved or deleted.

Insert point: click near the blue reference slot to split a curve without
changing its shape. The Points tab offers exact positions, handle offsets,
linked handles, Smooth point, Straighten next stretch, Delete and Lock.
Straightening a stretch can create direction changes at its neighbours;
align or smooth the adjacent handles when the checks identify such a join.

Undo/Redo works inside the editor. Escape cancels a drag, or leaves drawing
mode. Space+drag or middle-button drag pans; the wheel zooms. Fit table & track
brings the full design back into view.

GENERATE LANES
Set Total lanes (1-8), Your drawn lane (for example Lane 3), slot-centre
spacing and the side toward which higher numbers increase. Choose Apply lane settings. The blue reference slot stays exactly where it was drawn.
Left/right follows the blue drawing-direction arrow, not racing direction.
Reverse numbering changes labels and reference-lane number without moving
the physical paths. Racing direction remains the builder's separate setting.

TABLE AND CONSTRUCTION
Table & construction edits a rectangular or L-shaped table, edge-clearance guidelines,
preferred radius, board, slot, braid/tape dimensions and material allowance.
For an L shape, set the outer length/width, select the removed corner, then
enter its horizontal and vertical dimensions. Checks use the actual cut-out.
The same controls are available in the builder through Table / Space guide.
Its settings belong to this circuit. Existing routed sections keep their own
editor and construction profiles.

CHECKS AND SAVING
Open drafts and invalid geometry remain editable and can be saved to the
builder. Save & use in builder applies the work; then Save to Library keeps
a named design. The normal Open / JSON workflow restores editable anchors,
handles and lane settings. Use Edit FreeForm above the builder canvas or in
its left panel to continue.

Only a valid closed loop supplies lap routes and Live Preview. Folds, slot /
conductor crossings without sufficient height clearance, and abrupt tangent
joins block racing routes and invalid
construction geometry. Radius, table fit and table-edge clearances are shown
as planning feedback. Curvature jumps can be flagged even when directions
match. These checks do not simulate vehicle clearance or structural strength.

Start/finish is independent of the first anchor. Inserting a point preserves
its location. Deleting a stretch containing the timing reference clears it
with a message; set start/finish again in the builder. Changing physical lane
topology requires mapping review in Timing. Pure numbering reversal retains
physical lane identities and does not itself request remapping.

EXPORTS
Use the existing PDF, PNG, SVG/DXF and SlotForge buttons. CAD exports retain
the millimetre scale and table outline, with closed continuous slot paths for
a closed loop. Open valid drafts keep open ends; invalid CAD is blocked.
Reports describe unfinished geometry without claiming usable racing lengths.
SlotForge Timing needs the draft.8 shared runtime including freeform.js and
elevation.js; designs with assigned heights require draft.9. Shaped tables
or FreeForm squeezes require draft.10 and the table-shape.js runtime.

LIMITS OF THIS ITERATION
One FreeForm circuit per design, on a rectangular or L-shaped tabletop, up to 96 anchors.
There are no independent catalogue pieces inside that FreeForm design. It
saves to Track Library rather than a piece pack. Each cubic's local controls
must remain within the existing 10,000 mm sampling limit; large overall tables
can use multiple spans. A sampling budget limits overly complex drafts.
Arbitrary polygonal table outlines, 3D views and ramp engineering, automatic conversion of existing section layouts, CNC
toolpaths and board-cutting plans are later work.

HEIGHTS AND FIGURE EIGHTS
Choose an anchor in the selector above Lanes, then open Heights & bridges (useful
when two anchors overlap), enter Selected anchor height, and press Set height.
You can also select several anchors and set their heights together. Keep the
lower passage at zero and raise anchors on the upper passage. Deck thickness
and required clearance determine whether a crossing passes the check.
For example, a 150 mm upper surface over a 0 mm lower surface with a 12 mm
deck leaves 138 mm headroom on a level crossing. A ramp may have less headroom
at the edges of the crossing. Lengths remain plan-view values.

Heights interpolate linearly in each Bezier span's parameter. Adding an
anchor with Insert point preserves that height function. Changing points or
handles still changes the plan and can affect clearance. The controls do not
design a physically smooth ramp, choose supports, or check car-body clearance.
CAD remains a 2D projection; separate bridge construction must be designed.
There is no global bypass for unsafe crossings. Folds remain invalid at any
height. See HEIGHTS-AND-BRIDGES.txt for ordinary track-piece height controls.

FREEFORM SQUEEZE
Generate at least two lanes. Select the anchor at the start of the stretch,
then open Lane squeeze, set Tightest slot spacing and choose Squeeze next
stretch. It works on straights and curves. Spacing eases from the normal value
to the chosen minimum at the middle and back to normal at the end. The drawn
reference slot stays fixed. Existing routed sections still use Squeeze in
their section editor; Smooth midpoint / apex works on a straight too.

Insert point preserves an existing squeeze rather than creating two pinches.
Its split parts keep ranges of the original squeeze. Resetting just one part
can leave unequal spacing at their join; reset all squeezes or update the
neighbouring stretches together. Reducing to one lane requires resetting all
squeezes first. Geometry checks still reject folds and conductor collisions.

REVIEW REFINEMENTS (1 November 2026)
Far-away placed circuits use a local editing origin, shown above the anchor
selector when needed. Saving retains their world position, lane identities,
table and timing reference. Arrow-key nudges keep anchor focus. Failed edits
do not alter Undo/Redo history. Single-lane table checks respect the larger
of the first/last-lane edge-clearance requests.
