Routed Tracks - flowing-curve iteration (v0.3.9)
31 October 2026

Open Routed Tracks in the header, or choose Routed wood in the builder's
track-pack selector. A procedural entry is also in the Track Pack Library.
Load routed example to try a closed circuit, or start a new routed layout.

SAVE REUSABLE ROUTED SECTIONS
Create routed section now offers a destination pack, or a name for a new pack.
Save to pack keeps a reusable definition without placing it. Save & add to
layout does both. Saved routed packs appear on the Routed Tracks page, in the
builder catalogue, under Custom Track and in the Track Pack Library. They use
the same persistent storage and pack import/export as other custom packs.

LANE SQUEEZE
Select a routed section with at least two lanes, then choose Squeeze in the
Selection panel. You can also enable Lane squeeze when creating a section.
Choose Smooth apex to gradually narrow through the whole section, reaching
the smallest spacing only at its midpoint, then gradually widening again.
This is the default for new squeezes and avoids short entrance/exit ramps.
Enter the narrower spacing (for example 80 mm in a 100 mm layout), and whether
the bundle centre or a specific lane stays fixed. Both ends retain normal
spacing, so existing joins still match. The apex is this section's midpoint;
several separate sections are not treated as a single corner.

Existing squeezes keep Hold narrow spacing until you explicitly switch them.
That option retains the adjustable transition length and narrow middle region.
The maximum transition length is half the reference-path length. A longer
transition makes the narrowing gentler; short transitions can create tight
bends even on a straight. Red radius feedback is advisory; folds, crossings
and overlapping conductor footprints block creation. Check car clearance.

Circular corners convert to editable flowing approximations when squeezed.
Reset squeeze restores uniform spacing along that flowing path. Undo can
restore the original section after applying a change. Save to pack keeps the
settings and matching artwork for reuse. Lengths, materials, PDF, SVG and DXF
follow the generated geometry. Smooth apex Timing exports require draft.7;
Hold narrow spacing exports retain draft.6 support requirements;
older Timing runtimes must not import them as uniform lanes. Saved packs with
squeezes require the new pack version 2 (custom-library backups version 3).
The gallery reads installed disk storage rather than relying on a browser copy.

Use Add track piece to this pack to create another section in a routed pack.
New sections inherit that pack's routed profile; Copy & adjust starts from a
saved definition. Editing a placed section updates its layout instance only;
Save to pack can separately keep a reusable copy. Existing definitions stay
immutable. Storage errors keep the section form open, and pending writes lock
the form against accidental edits or duplicate submissions.

CIRCULAR TO FLOWING CONVERSION
Both Convert to flowing curve and choosing Flowing curve from a circular
corner preserve its angle, physical handedness, lane order, endpoint positions
and the first/last clearances on their original sides. The editor identifies
the result as Custom / converted curve instead of substituting a 90-degree
preset. A cubic approximates the arc; sampled lengths and minimum curvature
radii may differ slightly, with larger approximation differences at wide angles.
Keep the circular section when its exact circular geometry is required.

Saved packs retain an optional catalogueFlip boolean for their preferred icon
and insertion orientation. Mirror new pieces reflects relative to that saved
orientation. This is an editor-only pack hint: layout files contain explicit
placement flips and use the existing geometry and integration contracts.

The routed profile controls 1-8 lanes, slot spacing, two independent verges,
slot width/depth, board thickness, braid/tape width and gap, conductor and
adhesive thickness, braid recess, preferred minimum radius and waste allowance.
These are user-entered planning values, not construction standards.

PIECE OR TABLETOP ROUTING
Every new or edited section has a Routing type selector. Choose Individual
piece for a separate board section, or Tabletop routing for slots cut into one
continuous table. The Routed profile sets the default; each section stores its
own choice. Existing sections keep Individual piece behaviour until changed.

For Individual piece, the edge clearances define its wood outline. That outline
must not pinch or fold, even if the slots themselves are still evenly spaced.
For Tabletop routing, those clearances apply to the actual table boundary.
Imaginary individual-piece verges do not block the curve. Slots and conductors
must still remain valid. Add a Wood / MDF table to check board-edge clearances.
The preferred minimum slot radius is advisory in both cases.
An available-space guide is not a physical routing table. Choose Wood / MDF
table to enable board-edge checks. Tabletop clearance guidelines do not need
to contain the conductor footprint; actual slot/conductor fit is checked
separately. Individual pieces still require verges containing their conductors.

Invalid curve feedback identifies the failing lane-side edge and clears stale
radius/length values. A red fallback line is the middle reference path only.
The editor displays the profile used by that section, which may differ from
the defaults for new sections.

Straights use length. Circular corners use innermost SLOT radius, angle and
left/right direction. Slot radii increase by lane spacing. Artwork is generated
from the same geometry used for snapping, measured lengths and preview.
New section definitions are immutable; editing creates a replacement definition
so undo and saved designs retain their original dimensions. Profile changes can
apply to new sections only or explicitly update all routed sections in a layout.
Connected sections reflow from their old connection graph. A changed curve can
leave a closing gap; the editor reports that rather than silently stretching it.

The three editor display modes are complete surface, slots/conductors, and
slots only. Exports retain individual-piece surfaces; tabletop sections show
their slots and conductors on the separately drawn table. Optional tables use
the existing available-space guide: choose Wood / MDF table, set its size,
drag or resize it while unlocked, then lock it for building. One rectangular
table is supported. Table and routed profile data save with the design.

Planning checks cover chosen minimum radius, open/ambiguous ends, sampled 2D
surface overlap, table containment including verges, surface-edge steps at
joins and proud conductors. Unequal verges can step at a join even when slots
align; equal verges avoid this. The checks do not certify construction, car
clearance, banking or heights. Flowing sections can be joined into compound shapes. CNC output is
future work. The PDF is a fitted planning report, not a 1:1 cutting template.

Materials include total slot length and braid/tape on both sides of each slot,
plus the chosen allowance. Section surface area sums sections without overlap
deductions. Section counts are design elements, not physical MDF board counts.
Closed circuits retain actual per-lane routes and the normal live preview.

INTEGRATION
Non-routed layouts keep integration contract 1.0.0-draft.2. Circular/straight
routed layouts use draft.3; flowing curves use draft.4. Envelope stays version 1.
Timing must update its shared runtime, including routed-spline.js, and explicitly
support draft.4 for flowing imports. Read the draft.3 and draft.4 specifications
in integration/slotforge-timing. No Timing repository files were changed.

FLOWING CURVES
Choose Load flowing example to try a closed S-bend circuit. In a routed section
editor, choose Flowing curve / S-bend. Drag the two round direction handles or
the square endpoint. Arrow keys move a focused handle 1 mm; Shift moves 10 mm.
Exact coordinates are available in the expandable control. Start stays at the
local origin. Keep start and end directions lets you change the bend while
retaining tangent joins. Uncheck it to change their directions freely.

Presets: sweeping bend, S-bend and straight transition. A circular corner can
be converted to an editable flowing approximation with the same endpoints and
tangents. Its radius is no longer constant. Use several joined flowing sections
for more complicated shapes; each section currently has one cubic reference.

Bridge selected ends: select two routed sections with compatible open ends.
The editor fits a connecting curve and fixes its endpoints and
tangent directions. Drag the round handles along those directions to tune the
bend. Existing sections stay in place. Invalid folds cannot be applied.

CLOSE A ROUTED LOOP
In the builder, select the Routed wood pack or your own routed pack.
Open Routed tools beside the Sections heading for setup, closing tools and
the display selector. This menu overlays the catalogue instead of reducing
its height. Click outside it or press Escape to dismiss it. The + Section
button, search and section list remain directly accessible in the sidebar.
- Fill closing gap finds the two open ends of a single connected routed track.
  Review the proposed flowing section, choose a pack, then Save & close loop.
- Reshape to close changes an existing section instead. Select a section at
  either open end, review the proposed curve, then Reshape & close loop.
  Interior sections cannot close a distant gap while keeping the rest fixed.
Both tools preserve all other section positions. Endpoints and directions are
locked in the preview; the handles remain adjustable. Cancel changes nothing,
and Undo restores the layout. Reshaping keeps the placed section's identity
and lane order, without overwriting its reusable pack definition. Save to pack
can keep a reusable copy without applying the change to the layout.
The fitter tries several handle lengths, prefers the requested minimum slot
radius, and rejects folded/self-crossing geometry. Some layouts cannot be
closed with one valid flowing section: adjust the handles or the layout.
Check table fit, overlap warnings and minimum-radius advice after applying.
Automatic closure requires exactly two open ends and a connected layout of
routed wood sections. For several gaps, Bridge selected ends remains available.
Manufactured track is never stretched by these tools.

Every slot and conductor is a normal offset of the middle reference path.
Tight parts of slots highlight red against the configured preferred minimum.
Stationary paths, local offset folds and sampled self-intersections are rejected.
Artwork, per-lane lengths, preview, materials, PDFs and saved layouts use the
same adaptive samples (0.025 mm drawing/interpolation target). Lengths and
minimum radii are numerical planning estimates, not machining certification.

Timing-line position on a flowing section uses normalized middle-path arc
distance. Each lane offset reaches the same drawn cross-section, including
reflected/reversed sections. JSON exports retain points, tangent headings and
signed curvature. Sensor-based lap counting remains authoritative.

SVG / DXF CONSTRUCTION DRAWINGS
The builder and Export page now offer layered routed geometry in mm. Matching
surface seams are removed; slots and conductor edges follow their actual
offsets. Choose table, timing and scale-reference layers when useful. These
exports are geometry for a vector editor or CAD/CAM, not machine toolpaths.
See CAD-EXPORTS.txt for scale, coordinates, joining and format details.

REVIEWED EDITING AND EXPORT BEHAVIOUR
Changing defaults applies to new sections unless update-all is selected.
Existing imported sections keep their definitions, including template IDs.
Matching catalogue sections combine in the parts list; editing one section
still gives it a separate definition. Cancelled handle drags restore the
previous values. Planning checks refresh after a canvas drag is released.
Large JSON downloads may omit derived slot samples to stay within the import
limit; the embedded definitions regenerate those samples when reopened.
See ROUTED-REVIEW.txt for regression results and remaining verification limits.
See TABLETOP-REVIEW.txt for the follow-up routing-type review and repairs.
