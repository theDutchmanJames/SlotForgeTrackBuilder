App Theme settings
30 October 2026

Settings > App Theme contains the eight SlotForge Timing presets and a custom
palette. Presets use the shared stylesheet, with the Timing reference at commit
3719f61b8918491c58e385b4c19778df68b2c71f. Custom exposes six colour roles and
chooses white or black button text automatically. Contrast warnings identify
text/background combinations that may be difficult to read.

Custom starts from the current preset the first time it is selected. Use these
colours explicitly replaces the custom palette with a selected preset. Choosing
another theme or a quick header theme does not discard the custom palette.
Colours apply while editing without rebuilding the controls or changing track
geometry. Track artwork and PDF drawings retain their own colours.

Appearance is saved in this browser under slotforge.appearance.v1. Existing
slotforge.theme.v1 light/dark choices migrate automatically. It is separate
from track files and the disk workspace: another browser can have its own
theme, and clearing browser preferences can reset it. Same-origin windows
follow theme changes. Direct HTML links carry a validated palette because
file:// storage can be isolated by file. Unknown fields and non-hex colours
are discarded; malformed custom URL data cannot replace the current palette.

The Settings link is present in the builder, pack library and all ten galleries.
The generated catalogue templates include the link and required theme scripts.
