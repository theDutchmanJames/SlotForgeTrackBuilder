Appearance and geometry

Ninco’s rough black track, bright rails, broad connectors and red/white special-piece markings have their own vector artwork. Borders follow the historical red/white catalogue appearance; barriers and elevation hardware are separate accessories.

13 nominal road models use the published lengths and precise radius family. Road width and lane spacing are supported by a cross-manufacturer dimension drawing; slot edge offsets and single-lane width are inferred.

Borders, special routes, electronics and hardware have identification artwork only. Their full footprint or lane length cannot be calculated from these drawings. This remains a design pack, with no implemented app or importer.

Source issues and scope

  • Radius precision: Ninco publishes outer radii 241.37–961.37 mm. Comparison charts round to 242–962 mm; this pack retains Ninco’s values.
  • 10204 package quantity: 2012 lists six 100 mm borders; 2015 lists two. Quantity is null until the intended product revision is established.
  • 2012 catalogue label errors: The 200 mm border is mislabelled 10223 and taper set 10206 in the 2012 layout. The 2015 catalogue resolves them as 10203 and 10216.
  • Border footprints and finishes: 50 mm is a drawing assumption, so no border has a fit model. Red/white follows the selected historical catalogue; plain-black revisions are not verified here.
  • Digital assemblies: Catalogue photographs identify products, but do not fully dimension slot transitions, sensor positions, housing envelopes or kit inventories. These remain illustrations.

Classic 1:32 track, borders, N-Digital road devices and track-side accessories from manufacturer catalogues 2012 and 2015. Historical catalogue coverage; no claim of current manufacture or stock.

Outside this first pack: Raid and Snow surface variants pending dedicated appearance references; 10511–10515 extension kits including roundabout, dune and special curves; Older powerbase revisions not shown in the selected scope; Complete circuit sets, cars, controllers, power supplies and loose cables; Other Ninco track systems and smaller-scale toy ranges; Unverified plain-black border revisions.

Sources and data

  • Ninco manufacturer catalogue 2012, pages 6–7 — Manufacturer publication hosted by retailer. Visually inspected; contains precise outer radii, road dimensions and N-Digital pictures.
  • Ninco manufacturer catalogue 2015, pages 10–11 and 14–15 — Manufacturer publication hosted by retailer. Visually inspected for identities, quantities, road markings and accessories.
  • ScaleAuto manufacturer comparison drawing — Ninco width and lane spacing — Secondary-to-Ninco dimensional evidence: another manufacturer explicitly labels Ninco as 180 mm wide / 90 mm spacing. Its rounded radii are not substituted for Ninco’s precise catalogue values.
