Appearance and geometry

Modern SCX has blue-grey road, silver rails and open-loop connectors. This pack includes the shared Universal pieces, analogue power track, Advance devices, borders and elevation hardware.

All drawings are illustrative. Published straight lengths and supported curve angles are recorded, but modern width, lane spacing and radii remain unverified. No piece is enabled for snapping, physical-fit checks or lane-length calculation.

The community measurements you supplied are preserved separately. They distinguish 80 mm closing and 90 mm quarter straights, but the conflicting current SKU labels remain unresolved. R4 border appearance is provisional.

Source issues and scope

  • Physical geometry: Road width, slot spacing, radii, connector moulds and border footprints remain unresolved. All SVGs are identification illustrations; no snapping or lane-length geometry is enabled.
  • 80 mm versus 90 mm: 2026 labels U10323X200 80 mm, while the user-provided 2021 catalogue labels it 90 mm. Community notes identify closing 80 mm and quarter 90 mm as distinct, without resolving this modern SKU.
  • Community measurements: The user-provided forum excerpt helps establish curve angles and historical references. Approximate diameters are retained with their provenance, not converted into a supposedly exact nesting grid.
  • Border quantities: 2+2, 4+4 and the R1 +4 label do not establish allocation between shoulders, walls or end pieces. Unresolved counts stay null. STD units are carton quantities.
  • R4 border appearance: U10474X200 has a coming-soon placeholder in the 2026 catalogue. Its family-based illustration is provisional.
  • System identity: Modern Universal grey track is shared by analogue and Advance. The legacy-black adapter is analogue only. Hornby Scalextric and SCX remain separate systems.

Modern grey 1:32 Universal track shared by Classic/Original analogue and Advance, plus selected track-mounted devices, borders and elevation hardware from the 2026 catalogue. Catalogue coverage, including announced products; no claim of current stock or final release appearance.

Outside this first pack: Compact 1:43; Full legacy black-track catalogue; Earlier Digital/WOS electronics and electrical compatibility; Complete racing sets, conversion bundles, cars, controllers, transformers and spare car parts; Rally workshop tent and other decorative scenery; Historical 90 mm quarter-straight SKU mapping pending verification.
