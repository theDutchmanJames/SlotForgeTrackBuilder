Policar’s own appearance and geometry

Coarse black track, plain black borders, paired silver rails and separate white or grey guardrails follow the manufacturer photographs. Bridge supports use blue beams and black pillars. All artwork is newly drawn SVG.

The dimensional flyer supplies 179 mm double-lane width, 89.5 mm single-lane width and the nested radius family. The website gives 90 mm slot spacing. Centred slot offsets are derived: neighbouring modules consequently have a nominal 89 mm inter-module slot gap. Exact slot offsets and manufacturing tolerances remain unverified. Lane spacing stays independent of road width.

12 road models and 11 border models have published dimensional bases. Other drawings identify the product but cannot establish exact fit or lane length. No app or importer is implemented.

One specification conflict: P058

The R4 outer border is 11.25° in the 2023 dimension drawing and January 2025 catalogue, but 22.5° on the current website. The picture follows the PDFs; its geometry and automatic fit remain disabled until this is resolved.

Sources and coverage

Includes the modern track range, borders, selected power/timing assemblies and track-mounted accessories. P070 legacy-system powerbase kit, starter sets and loose electrical spares are outside this first pack. Retail quantities are separate from physical road counts; unresolved assembly counts are null.
