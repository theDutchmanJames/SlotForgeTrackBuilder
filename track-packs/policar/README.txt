Policar Track Pack — design draft 0.1.0

Open index.html for the gallery; pack.json contains documented definitions.
43 original SVG drawings and 43 retail products. 12 road geometry models and
11 border geometry models use published dimensions with derived details.

Published: 179 mm road, 89.5 mm single lane, 90 mm lane spacing, 60 mm borders.
Do not round 179 to 180: centred slots imply 89 mm between adjacent modules.
Slot edge offsets, tolerances and connector outlines remain unverified.
P058 angle is disputed: PDFs 11.25 degrees; website 22.5 degrees. No fit model.
Special-piece lane paths and accessory footprints are illustrative. Hardware
side views must never be interpreted as plan-view footprint measurements.
Unknown assembly counts are null, not zero. P010 supports add no road length.

See sources, scope and outstanding fields in pack.json. P070 legacy powerbase
kit, starter sets and loose electrical spares are outside this first pack.
Rebuild: node track-packs/tools/build-policar.cjs
Validate: node track-packs/tools/validate-policar.cjs

Design/research artefact only. No app, importer or installable runtime plugin.
