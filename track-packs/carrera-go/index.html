Appearance and geometry

Model scale 1:43; two-slot track. All physical dimensions are in millimetres.

Compact 1:43 road with paired rails, white edge lines and centre dashes. R1 and R2 road edges nest at 228 mm radius.

GO!!! is a distinct connector family from Carrera Evolution/Digital and Hybrid. Special-piece art is available through reference placement. Digital 143 electrical operation is not simulated.

Source issues and scope

  • Geometry basis: Lengths and identities use Carrera documents; width, spacing and R1/R2 road-edge diameters use independent published measurements. Exact mould tolerances are not established.
  • Special and elevated pieces: Crossovers, narrow sections, banking, loops, junctions and digital switches are reference-only; their actual paths and elevation are not replaced by straight/flat geometry.
  • R3: R3 identity/45-degree angle is published, but its road-edge diameter remains unverified in this pack; no buildable model is supplied.

Initial documented selection of road pieces and track-mounted accessories. Historical and current products are mixed; catalogue inclusion does not imply current availability.

Outside this first pack: Carrera FIRST; Build ’n Race decorative structures; Unverified aftermarket borders.

Sources and data

  • Carrera GO!!! manufacturer accessories
  • Carrera Zandvoort Park Circuit: manufacturer parts and lengths
  • Carrera 2013 international catalogue — Manufacturer catalogue archived by Carrera Club; GO special pieces, product photos and 61658 mixed-length retail pack.
  • Joe’s Carrera GO!!!: original technical measurements — Independent measurements: 114 mm road width, 60 mm slot spacing, R1/R2 diameters. Slot offsets are centred nominally.
