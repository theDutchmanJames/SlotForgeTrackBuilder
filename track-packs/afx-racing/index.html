Appearance and geometry

Model scale HO / 1:64; two-slot track. All physical dimensions are in millimetres.

Black plain road with narrow paired rails, small mounting holes and no Carrera centre dashes. Inch dimensions are converted using exactly 25.4 mm per inch.

Modern AFX / Tomy only. Vintage Aurora A/FX is not connector-compatible. Core flat roads support snapping and calculated nominal lengths. Special shapes remain reference-only.

Source issues and scope

  • Legacy Aurora: This pack models modern Tomy/AFX connectors. Vintage Aurora A/FX has different connectors and is excluded; no direct compatibility is claimed.
  • Nominal dimensions: 76.2 mm road width, 34.925 mm slot spacing and outer-edge radius convention use published owner observations. Centred edge offsets are derived; verify before a precision build.
  • Special pieces: Banked turns, hairpin, crossovers, chicane and terminal housing remain reference-only because their full routes or occupied outlines are not established.

Initial documented selection of road pieces and track-mounted accessories. Historical and current products are mixed; catalogue inclusion does not imply current availability.

Outside this first pack: Vintage Aurora A/FX and Aurora Model Motoring; Car/chassis spares; Unmeasured aftermarket borders.

Sources and data

  • AFX Racing manufacturer track range — Product names, inch sizes, angles, SKUs, retail quantities and appearance; includes page 2.
  • AFX Racing manufacturer straight tracks
  • A. Whorton: HO track building, January 2021 — Independent track builder. Explains modern Tomy and vintage Aurora connector distinction and the curved-track family.
  • Owner discussion: AFX/Tomy track dimensions — Owner-reported 3-inch width; named curve radius is the outer road edge. Not a manufacturer tolerance drawing.
  • Owner observations: HO track lane spacing — Owner-reported Tomy/AFX 1.375-inch slot spacing (34.925 mm). Do not substitute Tyco 1.5-inch spacing.
