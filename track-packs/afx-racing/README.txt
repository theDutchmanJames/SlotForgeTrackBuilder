AFX Racing / Tomy
Model scale: HO / 1:64; slotted; physical units: mm.

Black plain road with narrow paired rails, small mounting holes and no Carrera centre dashes. Inch dimensions are converted using exactly 25.4 mm per inch.

Modern AFX / Tomy only. Vintage Aurora A/FX is not connector-compatible. Core flat roads support snapping and calculated nominal lengths. Special shapes remain reference-only.

Measurement gaps:
- This pack models modern Tomy/AFX connectors. Vintage Aurora A/FX has different connectors and is excluded; no direct compatibility is claimed.
- 76.2 mm road width, 34.925 mm slot spacing and outer-edge radius convention use published owner observations. Centred edge offsets are derived; verify before a precision build.
- Banked turns, hairpin, crossovers, chicane and terminal housing remain reference-only because their full routes or occupied outlines are not established.
