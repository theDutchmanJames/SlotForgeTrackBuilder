SlotForge Scalextric Sport / Digital — first design pack 0.1.0

Open index.html to review 39 original SVG drawings and 37 product definitions.
The pack covers core 1:32 Sport/Digital track families and matching borders.
It is a design artefact, not an installed plugin: no app importer exists yet.

Appearance follows inspected manufacturer drawings and product images:
plain charcoal roads, silver rails, tan shoulders, red/white kerbs and grey
barrier edges. SVG artwork is original; reference product photos are not bundled.

Nominal road width: 156 mm. Nominal slot spacing: 78 mm, independently editable.
These are explicit planning assumptions inferred from the radial progression,
not independently verified mould measurements. Twelve basic pieces have nominal
lane paths and connector coordinates. Borders and special pieces are illustrations
with geometry=null. They cannot establish snapping, route length or physical fit.
Connector tabs are decorative previews, not measured mounting envelopes.

The pack preserves source conflicts. C8233 uses the current 350 mm listing while
recording the old catalogue's 345 mm entry. C8295 has conflicting lengths and
quantities and remains unresolved. Null quantity means unknown, not zero.

Scope: manufacturer catalogue 2016 pages 60–61, reconciled with available current
product pages. Full Classic, Micro, SCX, powerbases and newer set-specific pieces
are not included. Refer to coverage.json and pack.json for details and sources.

For pit kits, physicalTrackPieces counts two road components. Accessories count
four borders and three barriers. Do not count kit and contents twice. Pictured
border shapes group accessories schematically; their individual splits need work.

Scalextric (Hornby) and SCX / Spanish Scalextric are separate system identities.
Similarity in brand names is not proof of mechanical or electrical compatibility.
