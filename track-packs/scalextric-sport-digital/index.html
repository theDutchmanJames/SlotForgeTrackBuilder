A distinct Scalextric appearance

Plain charcoal roadway, paired silver rails, tan borders, fine red-and-white kerbs and grey barrier edges follow the manufacturer references. These are newly drawn SVGs, not reused Carrera icons or redistributed product photos.

The 156 mm road / 78 mm slot-spacing model is nominal and inferred from the published radial family. Border widths, connector shapes and special-piece routes remain illustrative. No piece is certified for exact physical fit. Lane spacing stays independently editable.

This first pack covers the core Sport / Digital catalogue families listed below. Classic, Micro and SCX are separate systems. Powerbases and newer set-specific pieces are listed as future expansion work; this is not a claim of complete historical coverage.
