Appearance and geometry

Model scale 1:32; two-slot track. All physical dimensions are in millimetres.

Historic black road with broad silver conductors, white edge lines and centre dashes. The manufacturer’s diagram provides three nested 45-degree flat-curve families: 3110, 3111 and 3112.

180 mm road width is published. Slot spacing is a clearly labelled 90 mm nominal assumption; do not treat calculated lane lengths as measured values. This is a separate connector family.

Source issues and scope

  • Slot spacing: The catalogue dimensions road edges, not slot centres. This nominal model assumes two centred slots 90 mm apart in the 180 mm road. Per-lane lengths remain estimates until independently measured.
  • Historical scope: This first selection follows the 1968–1969 catalogue. Later products, including 3123 lane crossover, 3124 hump, 3125 looping and 3154 banked bowl, are outside this pack.
  • Special pieces: Electrical connection housings, junction, chicane, banking and accessories remain reference-only; complete route and attachment geometry is unresolved.

Initial documented selection of road pieces and track-mounted accessories. Historical and current products are mixed; catalogue inclusion does not imply current availability.

Outside this first pack: Later Monte Carlo additions; 1:24 cars and electrical spares; Unmeasured aftermarket borders.

Sources and data

  • Fleischmann Auto-Rallye manufacturer catalogue 1968–1969 — Printed pages 41–43 (PDF pages 5–7) visually inspected. 3100/3102/3104 dimensions and 3110/3111/3112 road-edge radii 95/275/455/635 mm.
