Fleischmann Auto-Rallye
Model scale: 1:32; slotted; physical units: mm.

Historic black road with broad silver conductors, white edge lines and centre dashes. The manufacturer’s diagram provides three nested 45-degree flat-curve families: 3110, 3111 and 3112.

180 mm road width is published. Slot spacing is a clearly labelled 90 mm nominal assumption; do not treat calculated lane lengths as measured values. This is a separate connector family.

Measurement gaps:
- The catalogue dimensions road edges, not slot centres. This nominal model assumes two centred slots 90 mm apart in the 180 mm road. Per-lane lengths remain estimates until independently measured.
- This first selection follows the 1968–1969 catalogue. Later products, including 3123 lane crossover, 3124 hump, 3125 looping and 3154 banked bowl, are outside this pack.
- Electrical connection housings, junction, chicane, banking and accessories remain reference-only; complete route and attachment geometry is unresolved.
