Grey track with its own geometry

Medium-grey roadway and plain matching borders follow the manufacturer product photographs. Separate red and white guardrails come in three heights. Grid markings, customised print options and orange/white underside fixing links have their own drawings.

The published road is 200 mm wide, with 100 mm slot spacing and six radius families. Centred slot offsets are derived. Straight borders are dimensioned at 48 mm wide; curve borders use 48 mm as an explicit assumption. Exact connectors, tolerances and special-piece footprints remain unresolved.

15 road models and 15 border models are included. Illustrative special sections have no usable snapping or lane-length model. These are design assets; no editor or importer has been implemented.

Source issues retained

  • SC-10005 identity: Panel 07 labels its 200 mm road SC-10105, also used there for an R2 inner border. Panel 04 and the starter-set inventory identify the road as SC-10005; the latter is used.
  • SC-10001C / SC-10001D identity: Panel 06 shortens/mistypes the base codes as SC-1001c / SC-1000d. Normalized to the SC-10001 straight family, with letter suffixes; confirm commercial SKU before ordering.
  • Border colouring: Overview geometry has red/white kerbs, but standard product photographs show plain grey mouldings. Artwork follows product photographs; pit schematic and custom-print variants are separately labelled.
  • R1 half-angle pieces: Diagram items 25–27 have no unambiguous product-code mapping in the inspected panels and are excluded rather than assigned invented SKUs.

Digital and pit pieces are catalogue-listed designs; availability and fitted electronics are not established. Unknown retail quantities stay unknown. Complete starter sets, the paint marker and unidentified half-angle R1 parts are outside this first pack.
