ScaleAuto Pro Track — design draft 0.1.0

Open index.html. 49 SVG drawings, 48 product definitions,
15 road models and 15 border models.

Published road: 200 mm wide, 100 mm slot spacing, six nested radii.
Centred slot offsets are derived. Straight borders: 48 mm wide; applying this
width to curves is an explicit assumption. All exact-fit capabilities are off.

Plain grey track/borders follow photos, not the coloured schematic kerbs.
Special routes, housings, adapters and hardware are identification drawings
with unresolved full footprints. No lane lengths are inferred from those SVGs.
Unknown purchase quantities and assembly counts are null.

See sourceIssues and outstanding in pack.json for catalogue typos, half-angle
R1 exclusions, SKU normalisation and digital availability caveats. Sources
are current manufacturer catalogue panels, inspected visually.

Rebuild: node track-packs/tools/build-scaleauto.cjs
Validate: node track-packs/tools/validate-scaleauto.cjs
Design/research only: no app or installed plugin runtime.
