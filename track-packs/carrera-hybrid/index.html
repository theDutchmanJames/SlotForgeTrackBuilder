Appearance and geometry

Model scale 1:50; slotless surfaces. All physical dimensions are in millimetres.

Slotless black surfaces with red/white and blue/white edge strips, without metallic rails or guide slots. Standard straight: 433 × 250 mm; standard curve: 375 mm centre radius.

Click road pieces to build a visual layout with approximate magnetic ends. PDF exports include the layout and piece quantities. Slot routes, lap lengths and slot-car preview are not supplied. Red/blue edges distinguish left- and right-hand physical parts.

Source issues and scope

  • Slotless support: Hybrid supports approximate visual planning with magnetic road ends. Slot routes, calculated lap lengths and slot-car preview are not supplied.
  • Handed pieces: Left and right are separate physical parts with directional red/blue edge markings. They are listed separately; reflecting artwork does not make the opposite physical part.
  • Dimensions: Standard straight and standard curve envelope use manufacturer dimensions. R2 dimensions use the cited independent examination. Special surfaces / accessories have incomplete footprints and remain schematic.

Initial documented selection of road pieces and track-mounted accessories. Historical and current products are mixed; catalogue inclusion does not imply current availability.

Outside this first pack: Free-driving simulation; Digital slot-switch routes; Unreleased or unverified special geometry.

Sources and data

  • Carrera Hybrid manufacturer overview and dimensional FAQ — 1:50 scale; slotless; 433 × 250 mm straight; standard curve centre radius 375 mm.
  • Carrera Hybrid Track Pack 1 — Six straights, two left and two right curves.
  • Carrera Hybrid R2 / 30° curves
  • Carrera Hybrid R1 / 30° curves — Manufacturer lists this product as pre-order; catalogue presence is not availability.
  • Carrera Fun: own Hybrid track examination — Original examination with photos, part letters, 60-degree standard curve, R2 road edges, handed red/blue borders and directional markings.
