Build wider layouts with magnetic side snapping. Parallel straights align side by side, and matching adjacent curve radii nest together without changing their physical dimensions.

## New: magnetic road sides

- Use the existing **Snap on** toggle for both road ends and road sides.
- Straights align along compatible edges; nearby start/end seams align too. Shorter pieces can share part of a longer road edge.
- Matching adjacent curves, such as R1/R2 and R2/R3, snap to the same centre.
- A **cyan dashed edge** shows the side alignment while dragging.
- Rotated, reversed and mirrored pieces are supported. Selections move as a group, including closed circuits with no free end connectors.
- Hold **Shift** while dragging, or choose **Snap off**, for free placement. Undo/redo keeps working normally.

The app keeps different connector families separate and never stretches a curve to force it to fit. Reference pictures and borders do not act as magnetic road sides. Side alignment does not create electrical connections or change calculated lane geometry.

## Install or update

1. Download **SlotForge-Track-Builder-v0.3.3-complete.zip** and extract it completely.
2. If updating a running installed copy, choose **Storage → Stop local app** in that copy first.
3. Run **Install Windows.cmd**, **Install macOS.command**, or `sh "Install Linux.sh"` for your system.
4. Reopen the app using its installed shortcut or **Start SlotForge** launcher.

Your saved Track Library and custom packs are kept. The package includes all ten built-in packs, the source and the installers. The `.sha256` file is supplied for checking the download.
