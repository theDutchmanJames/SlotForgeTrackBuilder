A reliability update following a full review of SlotForge Circuit.

## Fixes

- FreeForm rejects excessive lane counts before allocating generated lanes.
- Returning to FreeForm refreshes a clean draft after the builder changes. Unsaved edits stay intact, with a notice explaining how to keep them or open the current track.
- Late storage responses no longer overwrite a reopened Save to Library dialog.
- Invalid FreeForm drafts keep their piece counts without reporting misleading zero lengths in PDF and CSV exports.
- Restoring a workspace keeps empty custom packs, their curve-family settings and library visibility. Repeated imports remain deduplicated.
- Height guidance now points to the correct FreeForm controls.

## Still a browser app

Circuit runs in your browser on **Windows, macOS and Linux**. The installers put the app and a small local helper on your computer. The launcher starts the helper and opens your browser; your installed workspace is stored in local files. Direct HTML use remains available and uses browser storage.

## Checks and compatibility

**370 application/installer tests**, **144 isolated Timing handoff tests** and **15 integration fixtures** passed. All ten track-pack catalogues validated. Targeted browser checks covered draft protection, lane validation, disk persistence and PDF download; the generated portrait PDF was visually inspected.

The layout envelope remains **version 1** and integration contracts are unchanged. The shared runtime and developer handoff are included. Native macOS/Linux installation, fresh Illustrator/Fusion imports and the separate SlotForge Timing app were not directly tested in this review.

## Install or update

1. Download **SlotForge-Circuit-v0.3.16-complete.zip** and extract the entire ZIP.
2. If your installed copy is running, click **Storage: disk** in the bottom-right corner, choose **Stop local app**, then close that tab. If saving has failed, download a workspace backup first.
3. Run **Install Windows.cmd**, **Install macOS.command**, or `sh "Install Linux.sh"`, then start Circuit again.

The installer keeps your saved Track Library and custom packs. Choose the complete ZIP for normal use. Source and Timing handoff ZIPs are for developers; each archive has a SHA-256 checksum. Personal workspaces are excluded.
